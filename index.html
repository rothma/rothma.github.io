Introduction

I am a researcher at the intersection of quantum computing and artificial intelligence. My research interests span from the application of machine learning techniques over quantum algorithms to quantum-based data processing for applications such as optimization and simulation. I currently head the Fraunhofer Research and Innovation Center Applied Quantum Artificial Intelligence in Heilbronn and co-lead flaQship — the Fraunhofer Lab for Applied Quantum Computing in Stuttgart-Heilbronn.
